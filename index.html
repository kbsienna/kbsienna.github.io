The Ailouran on Earth

eBook · PDF + EPUB · €5.99

Full-length novel · 93,000 words

The Ailouran on Earth is an erotic dark fantasy romance.

Kedi’s ordinary life shatters the moment she is kidnapped by a mysterious man. She awakens in another world, trapped at the center of a terrifying accusation and a past she cannot remember. Held captive and interrogated by Macan, a man who has already decided what she is allowed to be, she struggles to make sense of her new reality.

Macan is tasked with capturing the exile who was never meant to survive her banishment and dragging her back to Threa. Everything he has been taught insists Kedi is a dangerous seductress responsible for bloodshed. Yet no matter how cruel he becomes, the monster he expects does not awaken, and the certainty he relies on begins to fracture.

What begins as revenge and a mission to make her suffer turns into something far more volatile, as desire and resentment twist together in a place where vulnerability can be fatal.
