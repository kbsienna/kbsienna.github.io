The Ailouran on Earth is an erotic dark fantasy romance.

Kedi’s ordinary life shatters when she is kidnapped by a mysterious man. She finds herself in another world, at the center of a terrifying accusation and a past she does not remember. Labeled a dangerous seductress, she is interrogated, controlled, and punished by Macan, a man who has already decided what she is allowed to be.

Macan is tasked with capturing an exile who was never meant to survive her banishment and bringing her back to Threa. Everything he has been taught tells him she is responsible for the destruction of countless lives. Yet no matter how far he pushes her, she refuses to become the monster he expects, and the certainty he relies on begins to fracture.

What begins as revenge and a mission to make her suffer slowly turns into something far more volatile, as desire and resentment twist together in a place where vulnerability can be fatal.
